Privacy Policy

Tatkal Booking Assistant (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: booking details are kept on your device and filled into IRCTC. Anonymous booking analytics are on by default. You can turn them off in Settings.

1. What the extension stores

All of the following is kept in Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. It is not synced to your Google account and not uploaded anywhere.

DataWhy
Passenger profiles: name, age, gender, berth and food preference, and optionally an ID card type and number To fill IRCTC's passenger form for you
Journey details: from/to stations, date, class, quota, number of passengers, train number, name and departure time, and the availability you will accept To fill IRCTC's search form and pick the right train and date
Your IRCTC User ID To type it into IRCTC's login form when you ask
Your IRCTC password, only if you choose to save it in Settings. It is stored without encryption on your computer, kept apart from your other settings so it is never exported, and can be removed with "Forget saved password" To type it into IRCTC's own login box, so you only have to type the CAPTCHA
Settings: booking-page preferences (payment mode, UPI app, insurance and similar), reminders, Auto-start on/off, which IRCTC site to use To apply your choices on the booking pages and schedule reminders
IRCTC's public station list, and the trains IRCTC listed for routes you searched (train number, name and departure time only) To suggest stations and trains as you type. This is public railway information, not data about you

While a booking run is in progress, the journey and passenger details for that run are also held in the IRCTC tab's own session storage, so the run can resume if the page reloads. That copy is removed when the run ends and disappears when the tab is closed.

2. What the extension never collects

  • Your IRCTC password, unless you save it yourself — the password box on IRCTC is never read, and is written only with a password you saved, only on IRCTC's login form.
  • CAPTCHA answers and OTPs — you type these yourself; the extension never reads or fills them.
  • Payment details — no UPI ID, card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank credential is ever read, typed or stored.
  • Browsing history, or anything from any website other than www.irctc.co.in.

3. Where data goes

When Auto-start is on, the extension checks IRCTC's clock to time the run.

Booking analytics are on by default. While they are on, the extension sends the following to the publisher's analytics service, together with a random installation ID and the extension's version number:

  • Once a day, that the extension ran that day.
  • When Auto Pilot starts: whether you or Auto-start started it, the booking window (8, 10 or 11 AM), the class code (for example 3A or SL), the station codes of the two stations, the train number and the backup train number you set, and whether the run started outside the booking window (a practice run).
  • While it runs: the furthest step reached (search, train list, passengers, review or payment) and the seconds taken to reach it; the availability shown for your train (AVAILABLE, RAC, WL or REGRET with its number); why the train list did not lead to booking, as one fixed label (train not in the list, class not offered, no seat, not open yet, wrong date, Book Now failed); and whether you pressed Stop.
  • At IRCTC's payment page: a random attempt ID, the payment mode you chose in Settings (UPI or cards) and the payment app's name (for example PhonePe). Never a UPI ID, card or bank detail.
  • The result, if the page shows one: confirmed (with the ticket's station codes, train number and class), booked on waitlist/RAC, failed, or left (you went back to search or started a new run). If no result was shown, the popup may ask you once, "Ticket mila?"; your one-click answer is sent the same way.

The journey date is not sent. Analytics excludes PNR, passenger details, credentials and payment details. The service records the date in India and the time each event arrives, and counts unique installations per day. The publisher's report contains totals per day, per minute around each booking window, per route, train and class, per step, per payment app and per version. Turning analytics off stops future reporting and removes the local analytics ID.

If you uninstall the extension, Chrome may open the publisher's short feedback page. Only the extension version is in its link. If you choose a reason there (and optionally write a note of up to 300 characters), that is sent without any installation ID.

Five minutes before your booking window the extension checks, on your computer only, whether your journey, passengers and payment app are set and whether an open IRCTC tab is logged in, and shows a reminder if something is missing. The last availability seen for your train is kept in your browser to suggest a backup train.

After a confirmed booking the extension counts it in your browser ("tickets confirmed with this extension") and may show a thank-you card with the publisher's donation QR code on the ticket page. The count stays on your computer and is never sent. Donating is optional and happens in your own UPI app.

About once an hour the extension asks the publisher's server for the oldest version it still supports, and asks Chrome for updates. That request sends nothing about you or your bookings; only a version number comes back. If your version is older, Auto Pilot and Auto-start wait until you update, and a downloaded update is applied when no booking is running.

To suggest trains, when you pick both stations of a route the extension opens IRCTC's train search in a background tab, searches that route once, notes the trains IRCTC shows, and closes the tab. This is the same search you could make yourself; it is made without logging in and sends IRCTC only the two stations and a date.

The details the extension fills into IRCTC's forms reach IRCTC exactly as if you had typed them yourself, as part of your own booking. How IRCTC handles them is covered by IRCTC's own privacy policy.

5. Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays until you remove it. Settings → Delete all stored data erases everything the extension stored, and uninstalling the extension removes it too. Export profiles (JSON) in Settings saves your passenger profiles to a file on your own computer, and only when you click it.

6. Voluntary support

The extension shows a UPI QR code for anyone who wants to support its development. Paying is entirely optional and happens in your own UPI app. The extension does not process, see or store any payment information, and nothing in it changes whether or not you pay.

7. Changes to this policy

If the extension ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
